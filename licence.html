Version 1 · 9 November 2026 · Accepted on payment for any site licence (institutions) or organisation licence (firms, publishers, research houses and other entities). Individuals buy under the terms of sale.

In short

Pay for the band that covers your readers and the licence begins that day, for one year. Send a roster of readers; each receives a personal copy of the manual in their name within 5 working days, and keeps it. The institution may adopt SICM as its style, reproduce the forms in its author guidelines and use the CSL file; an organisation may also use SICM form in published and client work. Copies may not be shared beyond the named readers or used to train AI systems. Every copy is marked and registered so that a leak can be traced; a licensee that neither authorised nor could reasonably have prevented a leak, and reports it promptly, is not in breach. Indian law; disputes by arbitration seated in Bengaluru.

2. Definitions

Manual
The Standard Indian Citation Manual, version 2.0, including corrections to it issued by the Licensor during the Term, the SICM citation-style file (CSL) and the Chapter 13 declaration template.
Copy
A digital copy of the Manual issued by the Licensor in the name of one Authorised User and marked under clause 7. A printed library copy, where ordered, is a Copy issued in the Licensee's name.
Authorised Users
For a site licence: the students enrolled at, and the faculty, researchers, librarians and staff employed by, the Licensee, named on the Roster, up to the number in the Band. For an organisation licence: named individuals engaged by the Licensee, up to the number in the Band; a seat may be reassigned when its holder leaves, and the departing holder's Copy is withdrawn. Walk-in users of a library are not Authorised Users.
Roster
The list of Authorised Users supplied by the Licensee under clause 4: name and email address, one row for each.
Band
The maximum number of Authorised Users and the annual fee, as set out in the Schedule and chosen at payment.
Term
One year from the date of payment, and any renewal under clause 10.
Marks
The visible and hidden identifiers placed on each Copy under clause 7.

7. Marks, register and leaks

Each Copy carries, on every page, the title, author, copyright registration and licence notice of the Manual, together with the Authorised User's name, the Licensee's name, a copy number and the date of issue, and marks not visible to the eye. The Licensor keeps a register of every Copy issued: the Authorised User's name and email, the Licensee, the copy number, the date and a digital fingerprint of the file. The Licensee acknowledges the Marks and the register and agrees that they do not impair the use of the Manual.

If a Copy, or material bearing its Marks, is found outside the control of the Licensee and its Authorised Users, the Licensor will notify the Licensee, identifying the Copy. The Licensee shall investigate, report within fifteen days what it has found, and cooperate in tracing the Copy. A match between the Marks on the material and the register is evidence that the material originated from that Copy.

A Licensee that neither authorised the disclosure nor could reasonably have prevented it, and that reported it promptly on becoming aware of it and cooperated under this clause, is not in breach by reason of that disclosure. The Authorised User's own liability under their personal-copy licence is unaffected.

10. Term and renewal

The licence runs for one year from the date of payment. It may be renewed for further years at the fee published at the time of renewal, and the Licensor will write to the Licensee before the Term ends. The licence does not lapse for non-exercise of any right during the Term. On expiry without renewal, the rights in clauses 3(b) to 3(e) end and no further Copies are issued; Copies already issued remain with their Authorised Users under their personal-copy licences, and the Licensee may continue to use any author guidelines and course materials it prepared during the Term.

11. Suspension and termination

The Licensor may suspend the issue of Copies immediately on reasonable suspicion that the Manual has been made publicly available from a Copy issued under this licence, pending the Licensee's report under clause 7. For any other breach, the Licensor shall give notice and the Licensee shall have thirty days to cure it. The Licensor may terminate the licence by notice if a breach is not cured within that period, or without a cure period if the Manual has been made publicly available through a breach by the Licensee of clauses 6 or 8. On termination, no further Copies are issued and the rights in clause 3 end; Copies issued to Authorised Users who were not party to the breach remain theirs.

12. Fees

The fee for each Band is in the Schedule and on the pricing page, in Indian rupees inclusive of applicable taxes, payable in advance through Razorpay. A Licensee may cancel for a full refund at any time before it has sent a Roster or any Copy has been issued; thereafter fees are not refundable, including on termination for breach. A Licensee that needs more Authorised Users than its Band moves to the higher Band by paying the difference between the two fees, reduced in proportion to the months of the Term already run. Printed library copies, further workshops and lectures are priced separately on the pricing page.
