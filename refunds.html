Cancellation and refunds

Version 1 · 9 November 2026 · Part of the terms of sale and the licence terms.

In short

Every copy is made to its buyer's name, so the rule is simple: cancel freely before your copy is made, and after that a defective copy is replaced. Refunds go back to the method you paid with, through Razorpay, and usually arrive within 5 to 10 working days.

Digital copies

  • Before your copy is issued: cancel at any time for a full refund. Write to us quoting the payment ID on your receipt.
  • After your copy is issued: it cannot be returned or refunded, because a copy carrying your name is of no use to anyone else and cannot be resold.
  • Defective file: if the file will not open, is incomplete or is unreadable, we replace it. If a replacement cannot be made to work for you within seven days, we refund you in full.
  • Wrong name: if we misspelt your name, we issue a corrected copy without charge and withdraw the first. If the name was mistyped at payment, we issue a corrected copy for ₹99, to cover making and registering it.
  • Duplicate payment: refunded in full, as soon as we see it or you tell us.

Printed copies

  • Before printing begins: cancel for a full refund. Printing normally begins within two working days of your order, so write at once if you change your mind.
  • After printing begins: the order cannot be cancelled, because the copy carries your name on every page and cannot be sold to anyone else.
  • Damaged in transit, misprinted or misbound: write within seven days of delivery with photographs of the copy and the packaging, and we send a replacement at our cost. If a replacement cannot be made, we refund you in full. We may ask for the damaged copy back, at our cost.
  • Not delivered: if tracking shows that a copy has not been delivered within the times on the delivery page, we trace it with the carrier and, if it is lost, send a replacement or refund you, as you prefer.
  • Wrong name because of a mistake in your order: we reprint the copy at the published price for a printed copy less the price of the scholar edition, which you keep.
  • Wrong or incomplete address given at order: a copy returned to us for that reason is re-dispatched once you confirm the address, at the carrier's charge for re-dispatch.

Institutional and organisation licences

  • A licence may be cancelled for a full refund at any time before the licensee has sent a roster or any copy has been issued.
  • Once a roster has been received or a copy issued, the fee is not refundable, including on termination for breach. Copies issued remain with their readers.
  • Printed library copies follow the rules for printed copies above.

Lectures and workshops

  • A session cancelled by you more than seven days before the agreed date is refunded in full, or moved to another date without charge.
  • A session cancelled within seven days may be moved once to another date within three months; it is not refunded.
  • A session we cannot deliver on the agreed date is moved to a date that suits you, or refunded in full if you prefer.

How refunds are paid

Refunds are made through Razorpay to the card, bank account or UPI account you paid from. Razorpay states that a refund normally reaches the account within 5 to 10 working days of being processed, depending on the bank and the method of payment; we process every refund within two working days of agreeing it. We cannot pay a refund to a different account.

How to ask

Write to with the payment ID from your Razorpay receipt, the name on the copy and what you need. We acknowledge within 48 hours. Nothing in this policy limits your rights under the Consumer Protection Act, 2019.
