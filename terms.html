Every copy carries the reader's name, a copy number and the date of issue, together with marks that are not visible to the eye. The register exists so that a copy found where it should not be can be traced to the copy it came from. It is used for nothing else, and the privacy policy says how it is kept.

If a copy carrying your name appears outside your control, we will write to you first and ask how it got there, and you agree to tell us what you know and to help us trace it.
