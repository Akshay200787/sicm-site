Policy

Delivery

Version 1 · 9 November 2026 · Digital copies are issued by email. Printed copies are made to order in Bengaluru and sent by tracked post or courier.

In short

A digital copy reaches your inbox within 2 working days of payment. A printed copy is printed, bound and dispatched within 10 working days of payment, then takes 2 to 7 days to arrive within India and 10 to 25 days abroad. Shipping within India is included in the price. Nothing is stocked; every copy is made for the person who ordered it.

Digital copies

Your copy is made individually, marked with your name and copy number, and sent as a PDF, with the CSL file, to the email address you gave at payment, within 2 working days. Check your spam or promotions folder if it has not arrived; if it is not there by the end of the second working day, write to quoting the payment ID on your receipt and we will resend it. The download link in the email stays valid for thirty days; after that, ask and we reissue it.

Licensed institutions and organisations receive their readers' copies within 5 working days of sending the roster, each copy to the reader's own email address.

Printed copies

A printed copy is an A4 hardbound book, printed and bound in Bengaluru after your order, with your name on every page. Production takes up to 10 working days, after which the copy is dispatched by India Post Speed Post or a tracked courier and the tracking number is emailed to you.

Working days are Monday to Friday, excluding public holidays in Karnataka. Transit times are the carriers' usual times and are not guaranteed by us; if tracking shows no movement for ten days we open a trace with the carrier.

Addresses

Please give a complete postal address with PIN or postal code and a phone number the carrier can call. A copy returned to us because the address was incomplete or wrong is re-dispatched once you confirm the address, at the carrier's re-dispatch charge. We cannot change the name printed on a copy after printing has begun.

Damaged or missing

If a copy arrives damaged, write within seven days of delivery with photographs of the copy and the packaging and we replace it at our cost. If a copy is lost in transit we replace it or refund you, as you prefer. The cancellation and refunds policy has the detail.
