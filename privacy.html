Privacy policy

Version 1 · 9 November 2026 · Who holds your data: Akshay Sreevatsa, trading as Standard Indian Citation Manual, Bengaluru. Contact details are on the contact page.

In short

We collect what is needed to make your copy, send it and keep a register of it: your name, email, phone number and, for a printed copy, a postal address. Razorpay handles payment; we never see your card or bank details. We do not run analytics or advertising, we set no cookies of our own, and we do not sell or share your data. The register is kept for as long as copies issued under it are in circulation. You may ask what we hold, have it corrected, or complain, by email.

2. The register of copies

Every copy of the manual is issued to a named reader and marked with that name, a copy number and hidden marks. The register records who received which copy, when, and the fingerprint of the file. Its only purpose is to trace a copy that appears outside the control of the person it was issued to, and to enforce the licence under which it was issued. It is not used for marketing, not shared with anyone except as the law requires or a court orders, and not consulted except when such a copy is found.

3. Who receives your data

  • Razorpay Software Private Limited, which processes your payment and holds your name, email, phone and payment details for that purpose.
  • Google, whose Gmail and Drive services we use for email and for storing orders and the register. Google also serves the fonts on this site, and so receives the IP address of each visitor who loads a page.
  • GitHub, which hosts this website on GitHub Pages and keeps ordinary server logs (IP address, pages requested, browser type) under its own privacy statement.
  • India Post or the courier that delivers a printed copy, which receives your name, address and phone number.
  • Your institution or organisation, if your copy was issued under its licence: it knows you were issued a copy, because it named you.

We do not sell personal data, and we do not share it with anyone else unless the law requires it or a court orders it.

4. This website

The site is static. It sets no cookies of its own, carries no analytics or advertising code and does not track you between visits. The payment pages you are taken to are Razorpay's, and the LinkedIn sharing button opens LinkedIn's own page.

5. How long we keep data

Order and payment records are kept for as long as the law requires accounting records to be kept. The register is kept for as long as copies issued under it are licensed and in circulation, because that is what it is for. Emails are kept for as long as they are needed to deal with the matter they concern. Rosters are kept for the term of the licence and one year after it.

6. Your rights

You may ask what personal data we hold about you, have it corrected if it is wrong, and ask for it to be erased. We will erase what we are not required to keep; a register entry for a copy that is in circulation is kept until the copy is withdrawn, because erasing it would make the copy untraceable. If your name is wrong on your copy, we issue a corrected copy and withdraw the first. Requests go to .

The Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 applies to this processing to the extent its provisions have been brought into force, and this policy is intended to meet it.

7. Complaints

Complaints about personal data go to the grievance officer named on the contact page. We acknowledge a complaint within 48 hours and aim to resolve it within one month. If the matter is not resolved, you may approach the authority that the law provides.
